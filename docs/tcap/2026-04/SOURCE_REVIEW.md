## Not verified by this review

Executive Order 08 established Rental Ripoff Hearings and an agency report; it did not establish PET/TCAP or require asset creation. [NYC Executive Order 08](https://www.nyc.gov/mayors-office/news/2026/01/executive-order-08).

The [fiscal reconciliation](FISCAL_RECONCILIATION.md) checks the pilot multiplication and source-model assumptions. The exact midpoint of $128,000 and $200,000 is $164,000; $165,000 is a rounded planning input. The stated **30.8% ROI** cannot be reproduced from the disclosed capital and equity figures without a defined denominator and cash-flow model.

- The **$4 billion** combined annual spending figure, **80,000 distressed units**, and **$84,800** average family shelter cost need a defined period, denominator, and primary records before being presented as established facts.
- The **$21,800** TCAP operating cost, **$66,500** household equity, **30.8% direct ROI**, **6.2:1 social ROI**, **Year 12–13 crossover**, and **$165,000/$212,969/$254,167** per-unit scenarios are internal estimates. Their workbooks, assumptions, and sensitivity tests were not supplied in a reproducible form here. Do not describe them as verified or agency-certified.
- The executive brief and proposal contain inconsistent three-channel unit costs and equity values. Reconcile their tables against a single versioned workbook before any external use.
- The draft bill identifiers `INT 2026-A` through `D` are proposal labels, not evidence of introduced Council bills. Current legislative status requires a Council docket check.
- The legal implications of *Mother Zion*, Mitchell-Lama preemption, ULURP, and COPA/TOPA require primary text and counsel review. Do not state that these issues are resolved merely because a draft addresses them.
