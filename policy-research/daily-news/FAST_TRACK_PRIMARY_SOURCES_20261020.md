## Confirmed facts
- Charter amendment approved by voters Nov 2025; takes effect Jan 1, 2027; first cycle runs through Dec 31, 2031.
- 12 districts: Brooklyn 10, 12, 18; Manhattan 7, 8; Queens 4, 5, 10, 11, 13; Staten Island 2, 3.
- Review is capped at 90 days with no City Council review; applies to projects subject to Mandatory Inclusionary Housing in those districts.
