## Key finding
A09370A makes the cooperative/condo exemption turn on a 'consummated' plan, and narrows the regulated-housing exemptions to units whose rules already bar no-cause termination. For PET/TCAP, tenants who do not buy in must keep an explicit protection path, and affordability covenants should carry no-cause protection. See CSV rows R1 to R4.
